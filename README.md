## About

Kristopher Clark is a Solutions Architect at [ZEDEDA](https://zededa.com), where he designs and deploys edge computing infrastructure powered by [EVE-OS](https://lfedge.org/projects/eve/) — the open-source, zero-trust edge virtualization engine from the Linux Foundation. He bridges legacy brownfield OT systems with modern cloud-native Kubernetes workloads, helping enterprises run containers, VMs, and AI inference at the edge without ripping and replacing existing infrastructure.

With a career spanning VMware, DataStax, and OpenText, Kristopher Clark brings deep expertise in pre-sales engineering, distributed systems, and industrial IoT. He is an inventor, author on [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/), and a conference speaker whose talks have been featured at EclipseCon and JavaOne.

## What I Do

- **Edge Orchestration** — Kristopher Clark architects zero-touch provisioning and edge Kubernetes (K3s/K8s) deployments on EVE-OS for Fortune 500 enterprises
- **AI at the Edge** — Building and deploying on-device large language models and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines for real-time inference without cloud dependency
- **Brownfield Virtualization** — Modernizing legacy OT/IIoT infrastructure by running containerized workloads alongside existing industrial systems
- **Pre-Sales Engineering** — Translating complex technical architectures into business outcomes for enterprise stakeholders

## Active Projects

### 🦀 [OmniRAG](https://github.com/krisclarkdev)
A local-first AI tool built by Kristopher Clark with **Rust** and **Redis**, designed for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) entirely on-device. OmniRAG enables private, low-latency AI inference without external API dependencies — purpose-built for edge computing environments where data sovereignty and offline capability are non-negotiable.

`Rust` · `Redis` · `RAG` · `Local AI` · `Edge Inference`
