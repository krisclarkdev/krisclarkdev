# Kristopher Clark 👋

### Solutions Architect | Edge Computing Specialist | AI & Homelab Enthusiast

I'm a Solutions Architect specializing in edge infrastructure, distributed systems, and local AI deployments. With over a decade of experience across organizations like VMware, DataStax, and OpenText, I focus on bridging the gap between highly technical concepts and practical, scalable enterprise solutions. 

Currently, as a Solutions Architect at [ZEDEDA](https://zededa.com), I help large industrial organizations secure and manage their edge footprints. I design and deploy edge computing infrastructure powered by **EVE-OS** (the open-source, zero-trust edge virtualization engine from the Linux Foundation), helping enterprises bridge legacy brownfield OT systems with modern cloud-native Kubernetes workloads.

## 🚀 Active Projects & Engineering Focus

* **Enterprise Edge Computing:** Designing offline-first architectures, zero-touch provisioning, and secure K3s/K8s deployments at the edge for Fortune 500 enterprises.
* **Off-Grid Comms:** Building and deploying custom, solar-powered Meshtastic nodes using ESP32 hardware and custom-designed 3D printed enclosures.
* **Heavy Homelab Architecture:** Maintaining a robust Proxmox and TrueNAS environment dedicated to self-hosting local LLMs and open-source infrastructure.
* **OmniRAG:** A local-first AI tool built with Rust and Redis, designed for retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) entirely on-device, purpose-built for edge environments requiring strict data sovereignty.
